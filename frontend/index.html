    Review before publishing

    i

    Check the transcript against your original instruction. List every action, quantity, limit, safety step, deadline and exception. The worker must demonstrate every published requirement. These checks do not replace your site's permits or safety procedures.

    Correct transcription mistakes. A substantive instruction change needs a new voice recording.

    Worker understanding

    i

    The worker explains the task in their own words, by voice. AI compares this recording with every requirement. Unclear or failed checks keep work blocked. A passing check still needs both people to approve the same task version.

    Clarify the differences

    Discuss every discrepancy with the worker. Acknowledgment opens a fresh worker recording; it does not approve the task.

    Supervisor correction

    Explain the whole task again in a new recording, including the corrected details.

              HOW IT WORKS

              A clear handoff, step by step.

              1. Supervisor records and reviews.Record the instruction with consent, check the transcript, and publish all requirements.
              2. Worker retells on a second device.The assigned worker signs in and records what they understand, in their own words.
              3. AI compares every requirement.Missing, conflicting or uncertain details keep the task blocked.
              4. Supervisor explains corrections.Acknowledge the differences and document the correction. The worker must record again.
              5. Both approve the passing attempt.Each person approves the same instruction version and worker recording.
              6. Supervisor releases the task.Only a released task can proceed through this app's workflow. Follow all other required workplace approvals and procedures.

              An AI check supports communication. It does not grant a permit, certify that a task is safe, or replace required workplace approvals. Development and unconfigured environments cannot release work.
